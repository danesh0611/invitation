Sacred Thirukadaiyur

Thirukadaiyur is the holy abode of Lord Amirthaghateswarar and Goddess Abirami, where the young sage Markandeya was blessed with long life. Celebrating a milestone year here invites the Lord’s grace for health, longevity and peace.

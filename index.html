Sacred Thirukadaiyur

Thirukadaiyur is the holy abode of Lord Amirthakadeswaraswamy and Goddess Abirami, where the young sage Markandeya was blessed with long life. Celebrating a milestone year here invites the Lord’s grace for health, longevity and peace.

Legend says that when Yama cast his noose at young Markandeya, who was destined to live only sixteen years, the boy clung to the Shiva Lingam here, and Lord Shiva appeared to defeat Yama and bless him with eternal youth. It is also believed that the pot of divine nectar (amirtham) became the Lingam at this place, which gave the Lord His name.
